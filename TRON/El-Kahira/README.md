<h2>Designation Summary</h2>

<p>
  On <strong>July 23, 2026</strong>, the U.S. Department of the Treasury's
  Office of Foreign Assets Control added seven TRON addresses to the
  Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List entry for
  <strong>Zaid Issam Ahmed Al-Jebouri</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  Zaid Al-Jebouri is a shareholder of Türkiye-based
  <strong>El-Kahira for General Trading</strong>. Treasury states that
  El-Kahira transferred hundreds of thousands of dollars for Hamas and
  provided underground banking services involving both fiat currency and
  cryptocurrency.
</p>

<h2>Why This Matters</h2>

<p>
  These addresses are not inferred counterparties or provisional
  researcher attributions. They are official digital-currency identifiers
  published directly in an OFAC SDN List update.
</p>

<p>
  Treasury states that El-Kahira transferred hundreds of thousands of
  dollars for Hamas and operated underground banking services involving
  fiat currency and cryptocurrency. Treasury also states that El-Kahira
  serviced organized-crime groups and that Zaid Al-Jebouri provided
  underground banking services.
</p>

<p>
  Zaid Al-Jebouri was designated for owning or controlling, directly or
  indirectly, El-Kahira for General Trading.
</p>

<p>
  All seven addresses should therefore be treated as direct
  sanctions-screening and monitoring seeds. Their transaction history
  should also be reviewed for funding sources, consolidation behavior,
  stablecoin movement, exchange interaction and connections to other
  designated persons.
</p>

<h2>Attribution Boundaries</h2>

<ul>
  <li>
    The seven addresses listed by OFAC are direct blocked identifiers and
    may be threat-labeled as sanctioned seeds.
  </li>
  <li>
    A wallet that transacted with one of the designated addresses is not
    automatically sanctioned.
  </li>
  <li>
    Exchange deposit addresses, token contracts, bridges and service
    wallets should initially be treated as infrastructure or investigative
    pivots.
  </li>
  <li>
    Transactional proximity does not independently prove ownership,
    control, knowledge or intent.
  </li>
  <li>
    Counterparties should receive the El-Kahira or Hamas-financing label
    only when supported by separate attribution evidence.
  </li>
</ul>
