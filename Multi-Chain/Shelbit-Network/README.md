<h2>Executive Summary</h2>

<p>
On August 7, 2026, the U.S. Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control imposed counterterrorism sanctions on a network that included Siavash Kayvanpour, SHPS Shelbit / Shelbit Exchange, and Crypto Home DMCC. Reuters reported that Treasury accused Shelbit of processing millions of dollars in digital assets for Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps and other Iran-linked entities. The designation followed a Reuters investigation that identified Shelbit as a central hub in a roughly $4 billion Iranian sanctions-evasion network.
</p>

<p>
Reuters' underlying investigation found that Shelbit processed at least $4 billion since May 2024, including at least $125 million connected to Iran's central bank, and interacted with Nobitex and wallets linked by Israeli authorities to the IRGC. Reuters could not establish that the IRGC directly controlled Shelbit or the wider gambling network. Accordingly, this case treats the OFAC-published identifiers as direct sanctions-watch seeds while preserving a strict boundary around counterparties and analyst-derived graph expansion.
</p>
